## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an intimate, poetic, and heirloom-quality visual language designed for personal anniversary celebrations and sentimental digital keepsakes. It bridges the warmth of physical memorabilia—such as hand-pressed botanical paper, wax seals, and vintage polaroids—with contemporary editorial finesse.

The emotional tone is deeply tender, reverent, and quietly luxurious. The aesthetic blends **Tactile Glassmorphism** with **Editorial Romance**: layered translucent panels resting over warm, pearl-lit surfaces, accented with hairline metallic framing and gentle floating dynamics.

## Colors
The palette balances deep vinous warmth with soft powdery tones and reflective accents:

- **Primary (`#4A0E17`)**: Deep Burgundy / Vinho Imperial. Conveys grounded romance, intimacy, and high editorial contrast. Used for core interactive elements, key display headlines, and primary structural accents. Secondary burgundy tone (`#6A1B29`) serves interactive hover and pressed states.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`)**: Antique Gold. Used selectively for celebratory highlights, filigree badges, illuminated dividers, and interactive focuses (`#C59B27` for pressed; `#F3E5AB` for subtle luminous gradients).
- **Tertiary (`#E8A5B3`)**: Petal Blush. Softened rose tone providing organic contrast, chip selections, notification tags, and intimate decorative fills. Supported by atmospheric shades (`#FDF2F4`, `#F7D6DC`).
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm Pearl Ivory. Creates a warm, parchment-inspired base canvas that prevents the sterility of pure digital whites (`#FFFFFF` reserved strictly for elevated glass surface illumination).
- **Neutral Dark (`#2D1E20`)**: Deep Velvet Truffle. Applied to long-form body copy to ensure legible accessibility without breaking the warm emotional register.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm depends on the contrast between the literary elegance of **Playfair Display** and the clean structural legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Playfair Display**: Used for romantic headers, intimate date counters, chapter introductions, and pull quotes. Italics should be used selectively for sentimental emphasis, affectionate expressions, and chronological milestones.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Handles digital utility, interactive labels, long-form narratives, timestamps, and input controls. Letter spacing is widened on all uppercase label styles to evoke timeless editorial typesetting.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to 4 columns on mobile viewports. Sections favor generous negative space, allowing visual elements and love letters to breathe without crowding.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns, `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px), `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1200px` for storytelling sections to preserve typographic line length readability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px).

Vertical rhythms utilize rhythmic increments of `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (24px), and `space-xl` (40px) to establish deliberate, calm contemplation between narrative chapters.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is conveyed through warm, ambient luminescence and frosted glass surfaces rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Atmospheric Cast**: Shadows are tinted using burgundy undertones (`rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.06)` to `rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.12)`) blended with extended blur radii (up to 32px) to simulate sunlight passing through veil-like surfaces.
- **Frosted Glass (Glassmorphism)**: Backdrop filter blurs of `12px` to `20px` paired with high-translucency surfaces (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.72)`) bordered by semi-transparent gold or pearl outlines (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).
- **Metallic Edge Lighting**: High-tier floating components feature an inner top highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) conveying polished crystal or gilded stationery.

## Shapes
A balanced curvature system (`roundedness: 2`) softens the visual experience, evoking hand-cut deckle paper and polished keepsake items.

- Standard interactive controls and input fields utilize `0.5rem` (8px) border radiuses.
- Content containers, glass memory cards, and sentiment modules use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Featured love letters, milestone showcases, and modals employ `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Badges, pills, and date indicators adopt full circular profiles (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Burgundy (`#4A0E17`) fill, white pearl text, subtle antique gold hover border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.6)`), and an ambient wine-tinted shadow. Transitions include a microscopic vertical float (`-1px`).
- **Secondary / Gilded**: Translucent ivory background, 1px border of `#D4AF37`, text colored in `#4A0E17`. Hover triggers a faint champagne gradient wash (`#FDF2F4`).
- **Ghost / Narrative**: Text-only with an animated underline expanding from center in Antique Gold.

### Polaroids & Memory Frames
- White pearl paper backing (`#FFFFFF`) with generous bottom bezel spacing for handwritten script annotations.
- Slight organic rotational tilt (`-1.5deg` or `+1.5deg`) that straightens smoothly upon cursor interaction.
- Ambient diffused shadow with subtle inner vignette on photograph assets.

### Cards & Love Letter Panels
- Constructed with frosted glass panels (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- Framed by a 1px border colored in soft gold-leaf mist (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- Optional filigree divider accents separating chapters, dates, or milestone metrics.

### Chips & Milestone Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), pale rose base (`#F7D6DC`), with deep burgundy text (`#4A0E17`).
- Selected status reveals a subtle micro-badge or gilded border accent.

### Form Inputs
- Ivory fill (`#FFFFFF`), neutral border in `#E8A5B3`, transitioning to `#D4AF37` upon active focus.
- Placeholder styling set in muted warm truffle with gentle opacity.

### Interactive Floating Accents
- Floating audio player controls, countdown badges, and love letter seals use soft spring physics transitions with floating ambient animations (`y: [-2px, 2px]`).
